## 2025-05-14 - Accessible Focus States and Submission Feedback

**Learning:** Providing immediate visual feedback on form submission is crucial for MPAs where backend processing might take time (like video generation). Adding accessible focus states ensures that keyboard users can navigate the interface reliably. Using a `visually-hidden` label allows us to maintain the clean visual design while meeting accessibility standards.

**Action:** Always include immediate feedback (e.g., disabling the submit button) for long-running actions and ensure all interactive elements have highly visible focus states.
