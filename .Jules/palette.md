## 2025-05-15 - [Accessibility and Interaction Polish]
**Learning:** Initial UI lacked keyboard focus indicators and semantic labels for form controls, making it difficult for screen reader and keyboard-only users.
**Action:** Always implement a consistent focus ring (e.g., 3px green box-shadow) and use .visually-hidden labels for icon-only or clear-context inputs to ensure WCAG compliance while maintaining visual design.
